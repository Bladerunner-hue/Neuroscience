Music, Reward & Depression

A public-facing walkthrough of spectral biomarkers for emotional music processing in major depressive disorder — built on real BOLD from OpenNeuro, not mock oscillators.

Lepping et al. · OpenNeuro ds000171 · Source GitHub · Runs in-browser via marimo + Pyodide

How to read this book. Each chapter is a reactive marimo notebook exported to WebAssembly. First load may take 15–40 seconds while scientific packages install. Figures and tables use a processed feature store extracted from downloaded NIfTI runs (whole-brain mean BOLD, Welch PSD, event-locked peaks).

  1. Chapter 0 tSNR · flatness · IsolationForest

    QC Dashboard

    Run-level quality: tSNR, spectral flatness/entropy/SNR, spike fraction, IsolationForest outliers, collinearity, and a clean-run table for ML.
  2. Chapter I Cohort · paradigm · real BOLD

    Cohort & Design

    Who was scanned, what they heard, and how event files lock BOLD to musical structure. Full-cohort demographics plus processed whole-brain traces.
  3. Chapter II Welch PSD · valence · peri-stimulus

    Spectral Power

    Univariate Welch PSDs, band energy, and trial-type maps: what positive vs negative music and tones do to BOLD — with peri-stimulus waveforms.
  4. Chapter III Bake-off · best model · RecSys

    Algorithm Lab

    Spatial pseudo-ROIs, 14-model LOOCV bake-off, confusion matrices, explainability, and responder-score playlist priors from real expanded BOLD.
  5. Chapter IV Inventory · spatial · contrasts · PCA

    Features & Music Effects

    What data we have, harmonization, trial-type maps, spatial coherence features, subject contrasts, and PCA of the music-effect space.
  6. Chapter V TF offline · CNN · MLP · comparison

    Neural Net Results

    TensorFlow trains on a local machine; this page only shows precomputed STFT-CNN / MLP metrics, confusion matrices, and sklearn vs deep comparison — no TF in the browser.
